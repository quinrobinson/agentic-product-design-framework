Interview notes, 5 parents of youth basketball players (ages 9-14):

P1 (mom, 2 kids): "After games I have no idea what to tell him. The box score says 4 points, but he played great defense and nobody tracks that." Checks the league app once a week, mostly for schedules.
P2 (dad, coaches rec league): Keeps his own stats in a notebook. "I want to see if he's actually improving over the season, not just game to game."
P3 (mom): "The car ride home is the hardest part. I say the wrong thing and he shuts down." Would use something that tells her one positive thing to say.
P4 (dad): Tried two stat apps, quit both — "too much typing during the game, I missed the plays."
P5 (mom): "I don't care about stats. I care whether she's having fun and getting better." Wants progress in plain language, shares highlights with grandparents.
